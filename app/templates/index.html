Simple Workflow

How NatureLens AI Works

Three straightforward steps to reconnect with the natural world around you.

01
Step Outside & Observe

Take a stroll in your garden, park, or hiking trail. Notice an unfamiliar leaf shape, foraging pollinator, or singing bird.

02
Photograph & Identify

Snap a photo with your mobile camera or upload an image. Local open-weight vision AI interprets visible anatomy and provides educational notes.

03
Catalog & Touch Grass

Save the observation to your local journal, complete the reflection challenge, put your screen away, and continue enjoying the fresh air.

Open-Source AI. Real-World Discovery.

Total Privacy

Model Freedom

Zero API Costs

Offline Resilience
